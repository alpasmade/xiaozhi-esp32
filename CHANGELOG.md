# Changes in this fork

What Alpas Made changed on top of upstream xiaozhi-esp32, one heading per
day. The product's own changelog is in the Jakomotchi repository.

## 2026-11-06

- Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75 and 1.75C: the flight radar, a sweeping scope that lies over the face while a conversation is open. New in the board folder: jako_radar.c and its header (the widget, drawn like the face in LV_EVENT_DRAW_MAIN with its own 33 ms timer and integer geometry, invalidating only what changed), jako_radar_data.c and its header (the parser for the update's JSON and the seq gate, plain C with cJSON), and Inter SemiBold baked at 20 and 22 px for printable ASCII (font_inter_600_20_4.c, font_inter_600_22_4.c, with the Open Font Licence in font_inter_OFL.txt). The face gains jako_face_set_show: in ring-only mode it keeps the state ring and draws no eyes or mouth. The board registers two user-only tools, self.radar.update (data, at most 3072 bytes; returns the plane id in focus) and self.radar.close (seq), parses on the main task and draws under the display lock. The radar opens on the first update the seq gate admits while listening or speaking and closes on the close tool, on any other device state and after 60 s with no applied update; it sits just above the face, under the low battery popup and the preview image. The line holds while the face thinks, a turn to "No flight data" shows the face's error state for 2.4 s, the internal heap is logged every 60 s while it is open, and a long press of the small button moves the focus to the next plane, only while the radar is open. Click and double click are unchanged. The parser refuses data with text after the object, a seq below 1, or a src other than "feed" or "sample", and reads a name or type code of only spaces as none. Marks are drawn nearest first, as in the mock; a focus plane on the rim keeps its glow inside 197 px; wedge sectors the area being drawn cannot reach are skipped. kRadarCalmFace (off) calms the face alone while the radar is open, leaving the line turning. No shared files changed.

## 2026-11-05

- Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75 and 1.75C: body events. The face gains a happy body reaction (2 s above error, like dizzy), a body blink and a lean (jako_face_body_event, jako_face_set_lean). New in the board folder: jako_body.c and its header (plain C detectors for shake, cable in and battery full, tested on the Mac), jako_motion.cc and its header (the QMI8658 accelerometer on the shared I2C bus, probed at 0x6A and 0x6B, every call returning an error). The board polls the chip every 80 ms from an LVGL timer: a shake shows dizzy; stopping speech on a shake is written and off (kShakeStopsSpeaking). Cable in and battery full show happy, from the charging flags GetBatteryLevel already reads. A missing, misread or failing chip logs one line and leaves the device as before; kBodyMotionEnabled turns motion off. No shared files changed.
- Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75 and 1.75C: Jako's listening ring is the studio's neon #EEFF75 in place of the green #C7F23F (the listening entry of STATE_COLOR in jako_face.c, matching the face engine in the Jakomotchi repository). The thinking, speaking and error colours, the ring's geometry and its timing are unchanged.

## 2026-11-04

- Waking Jako: the 1.75C build entry carries a custom_wake_word object ("hey jako", English, threshold 20) and scripts/build.py understands it (the command recogniser with the English MultiNet7 model; an explicit --wake-word still wins; the values are checked in the generated sdkconfig; tests in scripts/tests). CustomWakeWord::Initialize fails loudly when the model is missing or the phrase is rejected and logs the derived phonemes on success; AfeAudioEngine then runs without a wake word instead of giving up the audio path. AudioService gains SetWakeWordAllowed, one gate for every caller; Application gains a stored voice wake switch (NVS namespace wake, key voice, default on) flipped by the 1.75 board's double click with a sound as the cue, replacing the echo cancellation toggle. The 1.75 board no longer powers off after five idle minutes (dim at 60 s only) and applies its first battery read.
- Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75 and 1.75C: Jako's face as an LVGL widget in the board folder (jako_face.c, its header, port header and heart masks), driven by the device state, the server's emotion names, the user's words, alerts, power save and the audio level of every frame played, in place of the emoji, status bar and subtitle. Display gains two no-op virtuals, SetDeviceState and SetAudioLevel; AudioService gains an on_output_level callback with the frame's RMS. No other board changes.
